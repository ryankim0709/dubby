Flashcards teach you words. Scenes teach you how people really talk.

From poster to premiere in five taps.

  1. Pick a scene.

    Swipe a deck of short scenes, each under a minute. Start on a bridge in Amsterdam, where Celia wants Thom to admit her robot hand freaks him out.

  2. Choose your role.

    Every scene has a cast. Play the character you like. Everyone else keeps talking, so you're always performing opposite someone.

  3. Hit your mark.

    See the line and its translation, get a three-beat count-in, and speak while the scene rolls. Your waveform lands right under the actor's, so you can see where you rushed.

  4. Get coached in character.

    After each take, the character you're dubbing appears as a hologram and tells you how your timing landed and what to try next. Then retry, or keep it.

  5. Post it.

    Your voice goes into the scene, music and all. Share it to the Dubby feed or straight to your Instagram story, and let your friends react.

Better with friends.

Every scene has two parts. Give one to a friend.

  1. 1

    Pick a friend.

    Record your character, then choose who plays the other one.

  2. 2

    They get a text.

    Dubby's agent texts them on iMessage with their part. They can text back with questions before they record. Built on Keiki.

  3. 3

    Watch it together.

    Both voices in one scene, with a score for each of you. Post it, or swap roles and go again.

Questions.

When can I use it?

Dubby is coming to iPhone. Founding members get in first, then the rest of the waitlist.

Which languages?

The first scenes are in English, with the translation in your language under every line. Tell us what you want to speak when you sign up; that decides which languages come next.

Do I need to be good at acting?

Not at all. The coach is about timing and speaking clearly, not drama school. Over-acting is encouraged, though.

Can I dub with friends who aren't on Dubby yet?

Yes. Pick them from your contacts and Dubby texts them their part. Their lines drop into the same scene as yours.

Who hears my recordings?

Your takes are yours. Nothing goes on the feed unless you choose to post it.

What do I need?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Headphones help the coach hear you over the scene.

How does the founding plan work?

Pick Founding when you join and pay $4.99 a month through Whop's secure checkout. That price stays yours for life. The free plan never asks for a card.
